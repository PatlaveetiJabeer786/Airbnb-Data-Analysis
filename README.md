## 🔴 Business Problem  

Airbnb hosts and investors face **critical challenges**:  
- ❌ Setting competitive prices without benchmarks  
- ❌ No clarity on demand drivers (reviews, ratings, location)  
- ❌ Hidden profitability patterns across neighborhoods  
- ❌ Seasonal demand insights missing  

➡️ Without analysis, hosts risk **lost revenue** and **poor customer satisfaction**.

---

## ⚙️ What I Did  


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║   🧹 Data Cleaning   |   📊 Exploratory Analysis   |   🎨 Visualization ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝


- 🧹 **Data Cleaning** → Removed outliers, fixed NaN values, standardized formats  
- 📊 **Exploratory Analysis** → Grouped & aggregated data with **Pandas**  
- 🎨 **Visualization** → Built colorful **2D & 3D charts** (heatmaps, boxplots, scatter)  
- 🧠 **Business Framing** → Linked every chart to a **real Airbnb decision**  

---

## 📈 Results  

- ✅ Identified **Top 10 neighborhoods** with highest ROI potential  
- ✅ Found **strong correlation** between reviews & booking frequency  
- ✅ Built a **pricing model framework** explaining **70%+ variance** in listing prices  
- ✅ Delivered **actionable insights** for hosts to optimize pricing
